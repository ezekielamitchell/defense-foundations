# Parallel Defense Curriculum

**Active teaching policy · September 19, 2026.** This supersedes the earlier Python-only preparation. Python, Rust, AI agents, battlefield AI and warfare are parallel learning tracks. P0 remains the only active phase; later curriculum modules are not promoted by taking an introductory course.

Public course pages were checked on September 19. These are selected fits for the current beginner baseline and assurance-first goals, not a guarantee of mastery. Account access, subscription coverage, enrollment and prices are unverified; no purchase was made.

Use one primary course per track. Parallel tracks do not mean collecting multiple courses on the same subject. Pause at the oldest unmet concept instead of following a calendar title past a prerequisite. Take short breaks inside the reserved studio.

## One-hour reading blocks

- **Python support:** [Python Crash Course, 3rd Edition — O’Reilly](https://learning.oreilly.com/library/view/python-crash-course/9781098156664/). Match Part I to the current course concept. Read for 45 minutes and spend 15 minutes recalling and explaining it.
- **Warfare concepts:** [MCDP 1, Warfighting — official Marine Corps edition](https://www.marines.mil/News/Publications/MCPEL/Electronic-Library-Display/Article/899837/mcdp-1/). Begin with the nature of war, uncertainty and friction as historical/doctrinal concepts; write a critical comparison, not an operational plan.
- **AI assurance:** [ICRC — AI and machine learning in armed conflict](https://www.icrc.org/en/document/artificial-intelligence-and-machine-learning-armed-conflict-human-centred-approach). Record the source’s claims, assumptions and human-control implications. This is academic study, not individualized legal advice.
- **Later targeted reference:** [Chip Huyen — AI Engineering, O’Reilly](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/ai-engineering/9781098166298/). The publisher labels it intermediate to advanced. Use a relevant evaluation chapter after basic Python and agent concepts; do not assign the full book to a complete beginner.

Alternate the doctrine and AI-assurance readings. There is no mandatory daily-paper quota.

## Agent implementation reference

[Ed Donner — AI Engineer Agentic Track, Udemy](https://www.udemy.com/course/the-complete-agentic-ai-engineering-course/) is the selected follow-on implementation reference. Its framework projects are not additional weekly obligations. Begin them when you can explain functions, collections, imports, errors and the agent loop without copying. The introductory agent track starts now through explanations and small exercises; advanced implementation remains evidence-led.

## Evidence and progression

Each session ends with one artifact, an honest blocker if needed, and an exact resume point. For code, record working directory, command, exit code, observed output and changed files. Course attendance is not mastery. Keep agent/research evidence separate from Foundation gates and independently governed company work.

Project 0 remains the main curriculum build. Separate toy agent exercises do not authorize weapons, targeting, live systems or company deployment. The [competency pathway](COMPETENCY_PATHWAY.md) still governs later dependencies. Personal timing and capacity come only from the current private Aegis authority and its generated projection.

If a paid resource is unavailable, use an official free reference for that same concept and record the substitution. Do not buy a course merely to satisfy a calendar row.
